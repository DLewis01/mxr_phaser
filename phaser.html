USING AN MP3

Select MP3, choose a local audio file, then press Start Audio. The music follows the same four-stage phaser path as the synthesizer. LFO speed, sweep depth, feedback and wet/dry mix therefore affect the music in real time. The file stays in your browser; it is not uploaded.

WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

Each all-pass stage changes the phase of different frequencies. The four stages are modulated together by the low-frequency oscillator. The phase-shifted signal is then mixed with the original dry signal. Where the two signals are out of phase they cancel, creating the moving notches characteristic of a phaser. A sawtooth input contains many harmonics, so those moving cancellations are much easier to see than with a pure sine.
